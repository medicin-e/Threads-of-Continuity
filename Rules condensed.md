# Official Rulebook for Threads of Continuity
### Version 0.7.1

---

## Objective

The objective of Threads of Continuity is to be the first player to weave three Tapestries of Reality. The game, though a competitive experience, will require working with your competitors to steer the game in a certain direction, for you cannot do it alone. The reality is slowly collapsing, and you must try to be the first one out of this reality by beating your opponents. Every action that each player makes will affect all players in the subsequent rounds. Maybe you want to sabotage your opponents, or maybe you need to form a temporary alliance to get what you want. Maybe you'll change the rules and win diplomatically. The universe is yours to shape how you wish, but no matter what you do, you must get out before it's all gone.

---

## Resources

There are five resources in Threads of Continuity, each with a distinct strategic identity:

| Resource | Abbreviation | Identity | Role |
|----------|-------------|----------|------|
| Threads of Continuity | ToC | **Flexible Currency** | General-purpose spend, conversions, utility actions. You can always do *something* if you have ToC. |
| Spatial Nodes | SN | **Infrastructure** | Primary currency for building and upgrading Production Nodes. SN is how you scale up and become powerful over time. |
| Temporal Bridges | TB | **Breakthrough Power** | Rare, premium weaving fuel. Hard to acquire, high impact. TB is what you hoard for your big moment. |
| Dimensional Ripples | DR | **Aggression** | Funds sabotage, hostile actions, and disruption. DR lets you mess with people — but it always comes back around. |
| Quantum Residues | QR | **Entropy** | The game pushing back against players. Mostly a liability, but can be weaponized. The residue of dangerous play. |

### ToC Conversion Rates

ToC can be spent in place of other resources at the following exchange rates:

| Conversion | Cost |
|-----------|------|
| 1 DR | 2 ToC |
| 1 SN | 3 ToC |
| 1 TB | 5 ToC |
| 1 QR | 1 ToC |

These conversions are performed as Weaving Phase actions (see §2.1.4.1).

**Important**: When converting, the ToC spent counts as usage of ToC. The gained resource counts as a gain of that resource (not as usage).

## Game Structure

### 1. Player Composition and Requirements

#### 1.1 Requirements for Play
- 3–5 players
- 1 notebook for each player
- 1 pen for each player
- 2 d6 dice
- 1 d100 die
- Methods for tracking ToC, TB, SN, QR, DR, and ToR
- Method for logging spent resources
- The Threads of Continuity Digital Tracker (recommended)

#### 1.2 Setup

##### 1.2.1 Player Turn Order
1. All players will roll both dice and multiply the numbers together.
2. Divide that number by your birth month.
3. Add your birth day to the result.
4. Turn order is determined in ascending order.
5. If the time is after 6 o'clock, play in reverse order.

##### 1.2.2 Player Starting Resources
Every player starts with the following:
- 5 Threads of Continuity (ToC)
- 2 Dimensional Ripples (DR)
- 1 Spatial Node (SN)
- 2 Quantum Residues (QR)
- 0 Temporal Bridges (TB)

##### 1.2.2.1 Resource Tracking
All players must track the number of each resource they have spent in a round. This number will be used to determine the Total Resource Usage at the start of the next round.

**Note**: "Lowering" a resource counts towards the spending of that resource.

---

### 2. Game Phases

#### 2.1 Game Phases
1. Preliminary Phase
2. Resource Collection Phase
3. Merging Phase
4. Weaving Phase
5. Reconciliation Phase
6. Ending Phase

---

#### 2.1.1 Preliminary Phase

This first phase sets the starting Reality Constant and the Weaving Coefficients. These values are recalculated once at the start of each round (before the first player's turn) and affect all players for the duration of the round.

See **Coefficient Formula** and **Reality Constant Formula** for calculation details.

Players may also opt to **skip their turn entirely** to reduce their QR by 10 (or by whatever QR they have, if less than 10).

---

#### 2.1.2 Resource Collection Phase

This phase focuses on collecting resources needed for the rest of the turn.

1. **Collect any resources from Production Nodes** you own. (This happens automatically in the digital tracker.)
2. **Roll the two d6 dice.** Based on the sum:
   - 2–5: Gain 3 ToC
   - 6–8: Gain 2 DR
   - 9–10: Gain 2 SN
   - 11–12: Gain 1 TB

---

#### 2.1.3 Merging Phase

This phase focuses on purchasing Production Nodes, exchanging resources, trading, alliance building, and sabotage.

##### 2.1.3.1 Purchasing Production Nodes
Production Nodes are crucial to mid- and late-game success. See **Production Nodes** for creation costs and details.

##### 2.1.3.2 Trading and Public Offers
See the **Trading and Public Offers** rules for more info.

##### 2.1.3.3 Alliances and Bribery
Players are encouraged to participate in alliance building and bribery during this phase. See **Alliances and Bribery** for details.

##### 2.1.3.4 Sabotage
Players may sabotage an opponent's Production Nodes.

- **Cost**: 3 DR
- **Roll**: d6 — on an even number (2, 4, 6), the sabotage succeeds.
- **On success**: The target loses half of all their PN output the next time they collect. The saboteur gains half of the lost output. Applied per-PN, rounded down.
- **On failure**: Cost is still paid. No effect.

**Example**: Player A has a Level 2 ToC Node (+2/round) and a Level 1 DR Node (+1/round). Player B successfully sabotages Player A. Next collection, Player A receives 1 ToC (half of 2, rounded down) and 0 DR (half of 1, rounded down). Player B receives 1 ToC and 0 DR.

---

#### 2.1.4 Weaving Phase

##### 2.1.4.1 Actions

The player may choose **up to three actions** from the following. Players may also **attempt a Weave** in the same phase (see Weaving Rules).

**Resource Manipulation**
- Spend 3 ToC (or 2 SN) to lower QR by 1.
- Spend 2 ToC (or 1 SN) to lower DR by 1.

**Conversions** (ToC as flexible currency)
- Spend 2 ToC to gain 1 DR.
- Spend 3 ToC to gain 1 SN.
- Spend 5 ToC to gain 1 TB.
- Spend 1 ToC to gain 1 QR.

**Player Interaction** (DR-based aggression)
- Spend 5 DR to force another player to lose 1 SN.
- Spend 15 ToC to insure your PNs from sabotage for 2 rounds.
- Spend 10 DR to force an opponent to lose 1 PN of their choice.
- Spend 7 DR to force another player to discard 2 resources of their choice.

**Special Actions**
- Spend 15 QR to trigger an Ethereal Chaos Event (ECE). This also requires the player to skip their next turn.
- Spend 10 DR to force the Reality Constant (RC) to increase by 3.

**Weaving-Related Actions**
- Spend 15 ToC to reduce the RC by 5.
- Spend 10 DR to halve another player's Weaving Score (WS) by 50% for their next weave attempt. This penalty persists across rounds until consumed.

##### 2.1.4.2 Weave Attempt
See **Weaving Rules** for full details on attempting to weave a Tapestry of Reality.

---

#### 2.1.5 Reconciliation Phase

During this phase, players must resolve any penalties:

1. For every 5 DR a player has, they must gain 1 QR.
2. If a player has **20 or more QR**, they have created an unstable timeline and must:
   - Forfeit their next turn
   - Lose 5 QR and 5 ToC
3. If a player has **10 or more DR**, they must roll a d6:
   - On a 5, an Ethereal Chaos Event is triggered (see Ethereal Chaos Procedure).
4. If a player has **15 or more DR**, they must roll a d6:
   - On a 1 or 5, an Ethereal Chaos Event is triggered.

---

#### 2.1.6 Ending Phase

During this phase, the player must:
1. Ensure all resources are accurate, including any penalties that have been resolved.
2. Update the **inflation tracker** based on resource production for this round.
3. Update any manual notes, alliances, and table trackers that changed.

---

### Game End Conditions

The game is won by either:
- Being the first to **weave 3 Tapestries of Reality (ToR)**, or
- Winning via **Dimensional Monopoly** (controlling >50% of all PN production for 5 consecutive rounds).

The game ends immediately if the **Reality Constant reaches 100 or higher**. At this point, all players lose — reality has collapsed from the overwhelming amount of chaos.

## Coefficient Formula

To determine the weight of resources and the coefficients thereof, players must first determine how much one resource dominated a round.

### Base Coefficients
| Coefficient | Resource | Base Value | Role |
|------------|----------|------------|------|
| α (alpha) | TB | 1.4 | Temporal Bridge weight (boost) |
| β (beta) | SN | 1.1 | Spatial Node weight (boost) |
| γ (gamma) | ToC | 1.0 | Thread of Continuity weight (boost) |
| δ (delta) | DR | 1.1 | Dimensional Ripple weight (drag) |
| ε (epsilon) | QR | 1.15 | Quantum Residue weight (drag) |

### Scaling Formula

Using Total Resource Usage (TRU) and Total Possible Usage (TPU) from the previous round:

```
Scaling Factor = ((TRU / TPU) - 0.6)²
```

**Adjustment Direction:**
- If Usage > 50%: Subtract Scaling Factor (overuse penalty — coefficient pulled toward base)
- If Usage < 50%: Add Scaling Factor (neglect bonus — coefficient pushed further from base)
- If Usage = 50%: No change

**Coefficient Persistence**: Coefficients carry forward from round to round. If a resource has no usage data, its coefficient remains unchanged from the previous round (it does not reset to its base value).

**Possible Usage**: The total possible usage is determined from all players' combined resource totals at the start of each round.

## Reality Constant Formula

The Reality Constant (RC) represents how unstable reality has become.

### Formula
```
RC = Previous RC + R^1.5
```
Where R is the current round number.

### Example Progression (Standard Mode, Base RC = 10)
| Round | R^1.5 (approx) | RC |
|-------|----------------|-----|
| 1 | 1.0 | 11.0 |
| 2 | 2.8 | 13.8 |
| 3 | 5.2 | 19.0 |
| 4 | 8.0 | 27.0 |
| 5 | 11.2 | 38.2 |
| 6 | 14.7 | 52.9 |
| 7 | 18.5 | 71.4 |
| 8 | 22.6 | 94.0 |

### Game Modes
| Mode | Base RC | Difficulty |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Casual | 5 | Easier — more time to develop |
| Standard | 10 | Balanced |
| Hardcore | 15 | Aggressive — fast collapse |

## Production Nodes (PNs)

Production Nodes generate resources each round. Players can create and upgrade these nodes to scale up their resource generation.

### 1. Creating a Production Node

To create a PN, a player must pay the required resources. The PN begins generating resources on the player's **following turn**.

#### Creation Costs (SN is the primary PN currency)

| Node Type | Cost | Produces |
|-----------|------|----------|
| ToC Node | 2 SN + 3 ToC | ToC |
| DR Node | 3 SN + 4 DR | DR |
| TB Node | 3 SN + 2 TB | TB |
| SN Node | 6 SN | SN |
| QR Node | 2 SN + 4 QR | QR |

### 2. Upgrading a Production Node

Players can upgrade PNs to increase their output. Each upgrade level increases output by 1 per round.

**Upgrade cost = Creation cost minus 1 of each resource** (same cost at every level):

| Node Type | Upgrade Cost (per level) |
|-----------|-------------------------|
| ToC Node | 1 SN + 2 ToC |
| DR Node | 2 SN + 3 DR |
| TB Node | 2 SN + 1 TB |
| SN Node | 5 SN |
| QR Node | 1 SN + 3 QR |

Maximum upgrade level: 4 (base level 1 + 3 upgrades).

### 3. PN Resource Collection

PN resources are collected automatically at the start of the Resource Collection Phase.

## Weaving Rules

### Weaving Score Formula

```
WS = (α × TB + β × SN + γ × ToC) / (δ × DR + ε × QR + baseDrag)
```

Where:
- **α, β, γ** are the boost coefficients (for TB, SN, ToC)
- **δ, ε** are the drag coefficients (for DR, QR)
- **baseDrag = 0.5 + (RC / 100)** — ensures there is always *some* difficulty, even with 0 DR and 0 QR

### Tapestry of Reality Success Threshold (ToRST)

```
ToRST = 1.5 × ln(RC + 1)
```

### Success Probability

```
PT = ceil(1.5 × (WS / ToRST) × 100)
```

Capped at 100%.

### QR Graduated Weaving Penalty

| QR Range | Penalty |
|----------|---------|
| 7–9 | −3% PT |
| 10–12 | −6% PT |
| 13–15 | −10% PT |
| 16–18 | −15% PT |
| 19+ | −20% PT |

### Weave Attempt Resolution

Roll a d100. If the roll ≤ PT, the weave **succeeds**.

- **Success**: +1 ToR. RC decreases by 2 next round. Committed ToC/TB/SN are consumed.
- **Failure**: Committed ToC/TB/SN are lost. RC increases by 1.

**Important**: DR and QR are used in the formula automatically from your current totals. They are **not** consumed by the weave attempt — you cannot opt out of their drag effect.

### Actions + Weave

Players may take up to 3 actions **and** attempt a weave in the same Weaving Phase.

## Inflation

Inflation is evaluated at the end of each round based on the **combined total of each resource across all players' inventories**.

### Inflation Thresholds

| Resource | Threshold |
|----------|-----------|
| ToC | 30 |
| TB | 20 |
| QR | 45 |
| DR | 25 |
| SN | 20 |

### Inflation Tiers

| Tier | Condition | PN Efficiency | RC Penalty |
|------|-----------|---------------|------------|
| Stable | ≤ threshold | 100% | None |
| Moderate | ≤ 1.5× threshold | 90% | +0.5 RC per 10 over |
| High | ≤ 2× threshold | 75% | +1 RC per 10 over |
| Severe | > 2× threshold | 50% | +1.5 RC per 10 over |

## Ethereal Chaos Events (ECE)

ECEs can be triggered voluntarily (Spend 15 QR during Weaving Phase) or involuntarily (high DR during Reconciliation).

**All ECE options require the initiator to forfeit their next turn.**

### ECE Options

1. **Nebula Flush**: Initiator loses all TB, SN, ToC. All other players gain 5 QR and lose 2 DR. RC +2.
2. **Nebula Reset**: ALL players (including initiator) reset all resources to 0 and lose all PNs. RC +10.
3. **Nebula Collapse**: All players gain 15 QR, gain 10 DR, lose 50% TB (rounded down), and lose all SN above 5. RC +5.
4. **Nebula Fracture**: Players split into two groups for 2 rounds (assign physically). Smaller group gains 5 QR each. RC +1/round for 2 rounds.
5. **Nebula Conflagration**: All PNs inactive for 1 round. Players with 4+ PNs must discard one. RC +4.
6. **Advanced Nebula Rescue Procedure (ANRP)**: Costly attempt to stop an ECE. Costs 8 DR + 3 TB. Roll 2d6:
   - 2–4: Failure — ECE proceeds.
   - 5–7: Partial Mitigation — ECE effects halved.
   - 8–11: Full Cancel — ECE cancelled. RC +2.
   - 12 (double 6): Ultimate Chaos Surge — ECE cancelled, but RC +5 and all players lose 25% of all resources.

## Dimensional Monopoly

### Win Condition
A player wins via Dimensional Monopoly if they control **more than 50% of all PN production** (outright majority, no ties) for **5 consecutive rounds**.

The streak is tracked automatically at the start of each round.

## Game Rule Change Proposal (GRCP)

Any player may propose a rule change during their **Merging Phase**. The GRCP process:

1. **Documentation**: Write the proposal (original rule, proposed change, reason).
2. **Initial Vote**: Supermajority passes immediately.
3. **Argument**: 90–120 second argument period.
4. **Deliberation**: Open discussion.
5. **Final Vote**: Majority or supermajority (as appropriate).
6. **Documentation**: Record the final decision.

**Any rule in the game can be changed under a GRCP.** This includes costs, thresholds, formulas, victory conditions, and even the GRCP process itself.

---

## Trading and Public Offers

### Trading
Write the trade on paper, read aloud, both sign. Counter-offers follow the same process.

### Public Offers
Write offer discreetly, fold, place center of table. Any player may view it privately. First to sign accepts. Each player only gets one look.

---

## Alliances and Bribery

### Alliances
Write an Alliance Proposal Form (APF) with: duration/goal, shared resources, break conditions & penalties. Send discreetly. Counter-offers allowed.

### Bribery
Write bribe on paper (any format), hand discreetly. Recipient signs 'A' (accept) or 'R' (reject). No counter-offers.

---

## Challenges

Players can challenge others' actions. Supermajority required to pass a challenge. Successful challenge: challenger must undo relevant actions.

## Game Modifiers

### Alternative RC Formulas
| Formula | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| Base RC + R^1.5 | **Default** — moderate growth |
| Base RC + R² | Faster — more pressure |
| Base RC + 2^R | Chaotic — exponential collapse |

### 2-Player Modification
- Reduce starting ToC to 3.
- Reduce PN creation costs by 1 SN each.
- Monopoly victory requires only 3 consecutive rounds.
