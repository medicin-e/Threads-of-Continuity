About the Game

Threads of Continuity is a game system, rather than a proper board game. Reality is slowly collapsing, and you must try to be the first one out of it by beating your opponents. Every action that each player makes will affect all players in the subsequent rounds. Although you are competing, you will depend on each player to be able to make it out alive. Use diplomacy, deception, and manipulation to your advantage. Change the rules as you go, create alliances, and sabotage your opponents. In the end, though, only one player will be able to make it out alive.
